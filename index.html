Welcome to the Valley in the Clouds

Araku Valley sits about 1,000 m above sea level in the Eastern Ghats of Andhra Pradesh, roughly 110 km from Visakhapatnam. Mist-covered hills, gushing waterfalls, silver-oak shaded coffee estates and the vibrant culture of the Adivasi (tribal) communities make it one of South India's most loved hill getaways.

Ride the famous Vistadome train through 50+ tunnels, walk into the limestone wonder of Borra Caves, sip Araku's GI-tagged Arabica coffee and watch the Dhimsa dance at sunset.

About Araku & Travel Information

Araku Valley lies in Alluri Sitarama Raju district, home to Bonda, Gadaba, Konda Dora and other tribal communities. Araku Coffee, grown by tribal farmers, carries a Geographical Indication (GI) tag.
